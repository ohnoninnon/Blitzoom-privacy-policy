BlitZoom Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

BlitZoom is a mobile application that helps users find words in game grids displayed on their device screen.

Information We Process

BlitZoom does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information for account registration.

Screen Capture

When you choose to analyze a game, BlitZoom uses Android's screen capture functionality to access the screen content that you explicitly authorize.

The captured screen content is processed in memory solely to analyze the game grid and find words.

BlitZoom does not save these screen captures to your device's gallery or to an application file.

BlitZoom does not intentionally upload or transmit captured screen content to BlitZoom servers, Google, AdMob, or other third parties.

Screen capture is only performed when you actively use the screen analysis feature. BlitZoom does not continuously monitor or record your screen.

Local Data

BlitZoom stores certain settings and usage information locally on your device. This includes information used to manage the number of free scans available to you.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to BlitZoom servers.

Advertising

BlitZoom uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information from your device and your interactions with BlitZoom and its advertisements.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or app identifiers
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google may use this information for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related services.

Data processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and settings.

The screen content captured by BlitZoom for game analysis is not sent to Google AdMob.

Privacy Choices and Consent

BlitZoom uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide privacy and consent choices where required.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy regulations, you may be asked to choose whether certain data may be used for advertising purposes.

You can access the available privacy options again from within BlitZoom.

Your privacy choices may affect how advertising is provided to you.

Third-Party Services

BlitZoom uses the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and related measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — privacy and consent management.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

BlitZoom is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information.

Screen content used for game analysis is processed locally in memory and is not intentionally transmitted to third parties.

Data transmitted by Google AdMob is protected in transit using appropriate security measures provided by Google.

Data Retention and Deletion

Screen content captured for game analysis is processed temporarily in memory and is not stored as a screen capture by BlitZoom.

BlitZoom may store local application settings and scan usage information on your device for the operation of the app.

This locally stored information can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling BlitZoom from your device.

Advertising-related data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies.

Children's Privacy

BlitZoom is not specifically designed for children. BlitZoom does not knowingly collect personal information from children for account creation or registration.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to BlitZoom, the services used by the app, or applicable requirements.

When this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning BlitZoom, please use the contact information available on the BlitZoom Google Play Store listing.
